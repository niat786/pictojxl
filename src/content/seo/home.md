## What is JPEG XL?

JPEG XL is a modern image codec for photos and graphics. Compared with 1990s JPEG, it often delivers about **60% smaller files** at similar visual quality. It also supports progressive decoding, wide color, alpha transparency, and solid lossless modes.

Teams pick JXL when one format must shrink bloated PNGs for UI and legacy JPEGs for photos. If you need a single private workflow, start here, then open the format pages for deeper guides.
