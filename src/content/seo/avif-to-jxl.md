## Why anyone re-encodes AVIF as JXL

AVIF is excellent at squeezing photos. Teams still standardize on JPEG XL when they want progressive decode behavior, faster trial encodes in the browser, or one format covering both photos and graphics. An **avif versus jxl** bake-off on *your* files beats any global ranking.

Treat both as modern codecs. Change pipelines only after you record size, encode time, and visual quality for the assets you actually ship.
