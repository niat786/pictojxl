## Why convert AVIF to JPEG XL?

AVIF compresses photos very well. Even so, some teams standardize on JPEG XL for progressive decode, faster interactive encodes in the browser, or a single archival codec across photos and graphics. An **avif vs jxl** test on your own assets beats any blog average.

Because both formats are “next gen,” migration should be deliberate. Measure file size, encode time, and visual quality before you change a pipeline.
